I used to think love was mostly intensity: the size of a feeling, the urgency of missing someone, the dramatic certainty that this person mattered.

Now I suspect love has more to do with attention.

Not surveillance. Not memorizing facts as proof. Attention as the decision to keep noticing a person after the first version of them has become familiar.

## Leaving room

To pay attention is to accept that someone can surprise you. It means resisting the comfortable habit of replacing them with the story you already wrote about them.

Maybe that is why love can be difficult. A real person keeps changing. Loving them asks us to remain curious without demanding that they become predictable.

There are louder ways to say “I love you,” but sometimes the most convincing version is simply: **I noticed. I remembered. I am still here, looking carefully.**

This is only what I think today. That is enough reason to write it down.
