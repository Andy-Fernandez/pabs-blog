Taste is not a possession you finally earn. It keeps moving because you keep moving.

At first, making things means borrowing obvious pieces from everything you love. Later, the references collide, lose their clean edges, and become harder to name.

Maybe originality is not the absence of influence. Maybe it is the shape left behind after many influences pass through one specific person.

I am learning to trust that shape before I can fully explain it.
