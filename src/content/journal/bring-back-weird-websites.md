The web becomes smaller when every page is optimized toward the same conclusion.

I miss sites with unnecessary rooms, odd labels, private jokes, abrupt color changes, unfinished corners, and links that feel like someone left a note for you.

Not every page needs to convert, scale, or establish authority. Some pages can exist because a person wanted a place for something they cared about.

That is useful enough.
