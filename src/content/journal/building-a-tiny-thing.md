There is a strange point in every small project where the thing already works, but I am still tempted to hide it until it can explain itself perfectly.

I am trying to stop doing that.

Shipping early is not pretending the rough edges do not exist. It is deciding that the rough edges are allowed to be part of the story.

## The tradeoff

Every extra system I add now becomes something I have to understand and maintain later. For this site, the useful question is not “what could it eventually need?” but “what problem do I actually have today?”

Today I need a place to publish. So I am building that place and leaving enough room for tomorrow to surprise me.
